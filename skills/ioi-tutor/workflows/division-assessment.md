# Division Assessment and Progression Review

Use when the student asks Gold/Platinum readiness, module graduation, or movement to the next division.

## Evidence gate

1. Read Notion Goals, Module Progress, Attempt history, real judge results, score/subtask evidence, Hint Max, prior exposure and Skill Profile.
2. Read provisional criteria in references/progression-rules.json. These are not official USACO criteria.
3. Separate CURRICULUM_COMPLETE, DIVISION_READY and OFFICIAL_ACHIEVED.
4. Construct an unseen, mixed-topic, timed test set. Verify positive evidence that problems were not previously attempted/read; unknown exposure must be flagged or excluded. Do not show topic category or Guide module link.
5. Use current or authentic archived judge evidence, with partial credit preserved. Do not grade from guessed outcomes.
6. Evaluate cross-topic transfer, retention, correctness/complexity proofs, contest strategy and coverage. Missing evidence → INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, not automatic failure or readiness.
7. Save Assessment and links to actual Attempts if Notion schema is installed. Update Goal stage only after documented outcome.
8. Provide targeted next steps in Training Queue. A Gold Ready result can support beginning Platinum without claiming official promotion.

Never turn number-of-AC, module percentage or stars into a formal promotion claim on its own.
